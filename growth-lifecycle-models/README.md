# Regional Incentive & Growth Strategy

I design reward systems that make partners happy while keeping the exchange profitable.

### Partnership Models
* **CPA (Cost Per Acquisition):** Good for quick growth. I set strict targets (KYC + Deposit) so we only pay for "active" sign-ups.
* **Revenue Share:** Good for long-term loyalty. This encourages partners like P2P merchants to keep trading on our platform for years.

### The Retention Funnel
I don't just stop at the "Sign-up." I use "Nudges" (like special local competitions and trading streaks) to make sure users stay active and continue trading every day.
